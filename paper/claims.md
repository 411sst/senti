# Paper claims: tests, results and how to present them

Each row is one claim in the paper's main argument. The test that decides it
was pre-registered in `results_fusion.md` (T30–T37, T39, T42) before it ran. "Results"
gives the numbers and the evidence IDs in `paper/evidence.md`. "How to present"
names the branch that applies, then gives the exact sentence for the paper.

Two checks were **not** pre-registered: T35's stay-cost stage and T38. Both
were added after the runs, have no pass rule and report no confidence
intervals, and both are labelled exploratory in `results_fusion.md`. Where
one changes the reading of a claim, a **Note** says so.

All MIMIC numbers are on the test split: 361,229 hours, 1,540 event stays.
The flagship is the two-head ALL-8 averaged over seeds 42 and 1–9. CIs are 95%
from 1,000 patient-clustered resamples. `paper/main.tex` is not edited here.

## Decisions for the authors

1. **Claim 7.** The pre-registered rule says the all-hours result passes. The
   exploratory stay-level check shows that pass is not a detection gain. The
   recommendation above presents it as a fail with the cost stated. The
   spec's pass sentence needs an explicit choice before it is used.
2. **Claim 3.** The spec's Fail sentence would say cadence carries part of
   the gain, but the data show it carries none measurable. The sentence above
   describes what the gap to the GBM does rest on.
3. **Claim 4.** The headline "6 to 9 points" is true only against the
   published recipes. Against the single GBM the margin is 2 to 5 points, and
   it reverses at equal quiet-stay cost (exploratory T38, no CIs).
4. **Claim 10 (death).** T39 fails, so by the pre-registered branch the
   death claim goes. `paper/main.tex` l.366–368 and the table row at l.387
   still state it and are not edited here. Whether the Mortality head is
   mentioned at all (with the parity sentence) is the authors' choice.
5. **Claim 11 (feed outages).** T42 fails, so robustness to a failed feed
   is not a claim. `paper/main.tex` does not make it today. If a later draft
   argues that separate agents keep the system working when a source fails,
   that argument needs the per-feed result and the alarm-flood finding beside
   it, or it goes. Whether the alarm finding goes into Limitations is the
   authors' choice.
6. **Wording that depends on these.** The title ("Validated on 208 External
   Hospitals") and the Discussion's first paragraph ("ranks deterioration
   better than any single model we compared", "6 to 9 percentage points",
   "carries to 208 hospitals") need the rewording above.
